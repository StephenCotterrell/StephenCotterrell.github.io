Engineer, Data Scientist, & Strategic Consultant

Stephen has a broad range of experience, spanning theoretical work to practical application.

Stephen fulfilled his goals of having a scientific career by pursuing a BSc & MSc in Chemical Engineering at the University of Cape Town. While completing his masters degree, Stephen tutored courses in advanced computational modelling techniques for Chemical Engineers, and it was during this time that he sparked an interest in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning.

Stephen has had an exciting extracurricular history too. While in High School, Stephen started competing in competitive puzzle solving. After a few years, Stephen was able to claim the South African National Record for fastest Rubik's cube average and single times.

Stephen also enjoys beer, and has applied his chemical engineering background to producing a wide range of exciting alcoholic products.

Contact Stephen if you are interested in procuring his services.
